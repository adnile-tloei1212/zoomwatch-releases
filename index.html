ZoomWatch

A free helper for Zoom meetings, made for people who use a keyboard and a screen reader.

What ZoomWatch does

ZoomWatch runs on your own Windows computer and joins a Zoom meeting next to you. It reads out what happens in the meeting, such as who joined or left, who is speaking, chat messages, raised hands and captions. It also gives you keyboard shortcuts for your microphone, camera, chat and other meeting tools.

ZoomWatch is a helper. It does not replace the Zoom app, and it is not made by or endorsed by Zoom.

Where your information stays

ZoomWatch has no online account and no server of its own on the internet. Your settings, meeting history and Zoom app keys stay on your computer. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Optional: back up your Zoom app keys to Google Drive

In Settings, under Zoom app keys, you can choose Back up to Google Drive. ZoomWatch then asks you to sign in to your own Google account and allow access. It saves one file, named "ZoomWatch keys.html", in a folder named "ZoomWatch backup keys" in your own Google Drive.

ZoomWatch asks only for the smallest Google Drive permission, which lets it see the files it creates itself. It cannot see, read or change any of your other Google Drive files. You can disconnect at any time from the same Settings page. Read the privacy policy for the details.
